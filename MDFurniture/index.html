Our Process

From log to living room.

  1. 01

    Timber Selection

    We handpick kiln-dried timber, checked for grain, moisture and strength before it ever touches a saw.

  2. 02

    Hand Joinery

    Mortise-and-tenon joints cut by hand — the same technique this workshop used a century ago, no staples, no sheet glue.

  3. 03

    Natural Finishing

    Multiple coats of natural oil and wax, hand-rubbed to bring out the grain — no synthetic lacquer.

  4. 04

    Quality Check

    Every piece is tested for weight, balance and finish before it's allowed to leave the workshop.
